# TradeFlow

**A small-business operating system built for service businesses and tradespeople.**

TradeFlow helps electricians, plumbers, builders, mechanics and other service businesses manage their day-to-day operations from one place — from the first customer enquiry through quotation, job completion, invoicing and payment tracking.

## 🌐 Live Application

**TradeFlow:** https://tradeflow-lime.vercel.app/

## 🚀 What TradeFlow Does

TradeFlow brings the core workflow of a service business into one system:

**Customer → Quote Request → Quote → Job → Invoice → Payment**

### Key Features

- Customer management
- Customer statements
- Quote requests and lead management
- Professional quotations
- Job tracking and management
- Invoice creation and management
- Payment recording
- Business profiles
- Public marketplace presence
- Customer reviews and reputation
- Business verification workflows
- Contextual onboarding and Help Centre
- TradeFlow Early Access
- AI-assisted business features

## 💡 Why I Built TradeFlow

Many small service businesses still manage customers, quotations, jobs and payments across WhatsApp messages, notebooks and disconnected tools.

I built TradeFlow to explore how these workflows can be brought together into a practical, simple system designed around the way tradespeople and small service businesses actually work.
